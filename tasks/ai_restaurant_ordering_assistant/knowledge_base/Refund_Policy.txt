Refund Policy
Version 1.0 - Effective 2026-01-01

Eligibility:
Refunds are available for orders cancelled before preparation begins
(CONFIRMED status only). Orders in PREPARING, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, or
DELIVERED status are not eligible for cancellation or refund.

Process:
1. Customer requests cancellation through chat or the website.
2. The system verifies the order is in a cancellable status.
3. Stock is restored and the refund is initiated.
4. Refunds appear within 3-5 business days.

Partial refunds are not issued for partially consumed orders.
