Restaurant Policy
Version 1.0 - Effective 2026-01-01

Cancellation policy:
Orders may be cancelled before preparation begins. Once an order moves to
PREPARING status, it cannot be cancelled. When an order is cancelled, all
reserved portions are restored to inventory immediately.

Refund policy:
Cancelled orders are refunded to the original payment method. Refunds are
processed within 3-5 business days.

Allergen notice:
We never guarantee allergen safety. Our kitchen handles common allergens and
cross-contact cannot be ruled out. Customers with severe allergies should
order at their own risk.
