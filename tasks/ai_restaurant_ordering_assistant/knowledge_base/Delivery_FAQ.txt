Delivery FAQ
Version 1.0 - Effective 2026-01-01

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Most deliveries arrive within 45-60 minutes during peak hours, and 30-45
minutes during off-peak hours.

Q: What areas do you deliver to?
A: We deliver within a 5 km radius of the restaurant. Delivery is free for
orders over INR 500.

Q: Can I track my order?
A: Yes. Once your order is OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, you can check its status in
"My Orders" or ask the chatbot.

Q: What if my order is late?
A: If your order is more than 30 minutes past the estimated time, contact
us and we will prioritize your delivery or issue a refund.

Q: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
A: Address changes are only possible before the order enters PREPARING
status. Contact us immediately if you need to change your address.
